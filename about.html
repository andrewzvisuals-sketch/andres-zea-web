Who I am

I'm Andrés Zea, a video editor and motion graphics designer based in Colombia. With 5+ years of experience, I specialize in creating engaging content for YouTube creators, brands and businesses.

My work sits at the intersection of storytelling and technical precision — I believe every cut, every transition and every sound choice contributes to the emotional impact of a piece.

"Video editing that moves people."
